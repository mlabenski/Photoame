## The short version

- Photoame exists to let you send disappearing photos to **one** partner.
- We collect only what the app needs to work. **We do not sell your data, we do
  not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or
  websites.**
- Photos are meant to disappear. A snap is deleted after it is viewed (plus its
  one allowed replay) or when it expires, unless **both** you and your partner
  choose to keep it.
- You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete account**,
  which permanently removes your data.

## Information we collect

We only collect information needed to operate the app.

### Information you provide
- **Sign in with Apple.** Photoame's only sign-in method is Sign in with Apple.
  Apple gives us a unique account identifier so we can recognize you. You may
  choose to share or hide your name and email through Apple; if you hide your
  email, Apple relays messages through a private address.
- **Display name.** The name you choose for your partner to see.
- **Age confirmation.** A record that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when.
- **Photos and captions ("snaps").** The photos and optional captions you send
  to your partner. Photoame does not save these to your device's photo library.
- **Reports.** If you report your partner, we store the report (reason, any
  detail you add, and identifiers for the people and snap involved) so our team
  can review it.

### Information collected automatically
- **Push notification token.** A device token from Apple Push Notification
  service so we can notify you when a snap arrives.
- **Crash diagnostics.** We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash
  reports (for example device model, operating-system version, and diagnostic
  logs) so we can fix bugs. This data is used only to improve reliability.
- **Basic operational data.** Records needed to run the service, such as your
  pairing status, snap delivery/open/keep state, and timestamps.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location, browsing history, or
advertising identifiers, and Photoame contains no third-party advertising or
cross-app tracking.

## How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- create and secure your account and authenticate you;
- deliver snaps to your one paired partner and notify you of new snaps;
- enforce the app's rules (view-once, one replay, mutual "keep", pairing);
- keep you safe (process reports and blocks, and apply screenshot notices);
- diagnose crashes and improve the app; and
- comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell
or rent it.

## How snaps work and how long we keep data

- **Snaps are ephemeral.** A snap is deleted from our servers after it is viewed
  (plus its single allowed replay) or after it expires, whichever comes first. A
  scheduled cleanup process also removes expired snaps.
- **Kept snaps.** A snap is retained only if **both** people choose "Keep". A
  kept photo is saved inside the app (in the "Us" gallery), including a copy
  stored on your device. Ending the pair or deleting your account removes it.
- **Ending a pair.** Ending a pair deletes all snaps shared in that pair for
  both people.
- **Account data** (identifier, display name, age confirmation, pairing state)
  is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account.
- **Reports** may be retained after review for safety, legal, and
  abuse-prevention purposes.
- **Crash diagnostics** are retained according to Firebase Crashlytics'
  standard retention.

## How your information is shared

We share information only with service providers that operate the app on our
behalf, and only as needed:

- **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** — authentication, database (Firestore),
  photo storage (Cloud Storage), server logic (Cloud Functions), push delivery
  (Cloud Messaging), and crash reporting (Crashlytics). Google processes this
  data on our behalf as our service provider.
- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple (authentication) and the Apple Push
  Notification service (notifications).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights,
safety, and security of our users and the service.

We do **not** sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## Safety: reporting and blocking

Photoame provides tools to keep you safe from another user:

- **Report** — from Settings or directly while viewing a snap, you can report
  your partner. Reports are reviewed by our team; we aim to review reports
  promptly (within 24 hours) and act on violations.
- **Block** — blocking ends your pair, deletes shared snaps, and permanently
  prevents pairing with that person again.
- **Screenshot notice** — if a snap is screenshotted, the sender is notified.

We do not tolerate harassment, unwanted nudity, impersonation, or other abusive
content. Reported content and accounts may be removed and offending users
banned.

- **Depending on where you live** (for example the EEA/UK or California), you may
